Terrestrial AI Compute [00:19:02]

$35B Cost → $50B/Yr Rent

While rivals spend $50B–$60B over 2.5 years to build 1 GW of AI compute and lease it for ~$12B/yr, SpaceX/xAI builds 1 GW in 8–9 months for $35B and rents excess capacity for $50B/yr (~143% annual cash yield).

Agentic AI & Starlink [00:46:44]

100,000 Satellites for AI

Autonomous AI agents will generate 1,000x human internet traffic within 5 years. Starlink will expand from 11,000 to 100,000 satellites with V3 (100x total capacity) and V4 (10 Tbps laser links).

Starship Economics [00:36:20]

Under $100/kg to Orbit

Full rapid reusability (10,000+ flights/year) drops payload launch costs from $1,500–$2,500/kg on Falcon 9 to under $100/kg, making solar-powered orbital data centers vastly cheaper than Earth power grids.

"Star Mind" Stacking [00:50:25]

$1T–$5T Added Every Year

Launching 100 GW/yr of Nvidia co-designed "Star Mind" satellites starting Q3 2027 adds $1T to $5T in recurring revenue per year. Since satellites last ~5 years, annual cohorts stack cumulatively.
